Chord Scale Maker · editor proposal · mockup B (recommended)

Bar grid editor: bars as cells, with an inspector

The chart is edited as bars, the way a player reads it: four bars a line, each section on its own line, the way iReal Pro and the Changes sheet both show it. You type chords straight into bars; the bar numbers, the sections and the directives are worked out for you. The selected chord's scale and function sit in an inspector beside the grid on desktop, and in a bottom sheet on phones. The text stays, as a Source tab. Static mockup: Catalyst look, the site's palette; the numbered dots are notes.

Below this box: the toolbar and the live Changes sheet, as today. The selected bar is outlined on the sheet too, and tapping a chord on the sheet selects it here.

  1. 1 Sections are lines. A section's name and key area head its bars; rename it once (click the chip) instead of in every row. No bar numbers are typed anywhere: they're counted.
  2. 2 Four bars a line, as on the Changes sheet, so the form reads at a glance; 32 bars fit in one screen with no inner scroll box.
  3. 3 @copy is a real thing on the grid: “A2 repeats A1”, with Show bars and Write out. Today the grid says “(edit in text)”.
  4. 4 Selection. One bar outlined, one chord filled. Typing replaces it; Space adds a second chord to the bar (two chords split it 2 + 2, as the chart already means); Tab goes on. Entering a tune is: type, Tab, type, Tab.
  5. 5 Structure buttons under the grid: add a bar or section, repeat a section (writes @copy), paste a line like | Cm7 | F7 | Bm7 E7 | from iReal, a forum or another chart.
  6. 6 The keys are shown, not hidden: the whole editor works without a mouse, in grid order (arrows inside the grid, one Tab stop in and out, not eight a row).
  7. 7 Inspector for the selected chord. Scale as a list you can read (default, inside, outside, any scale by search: no modal with two selects); function with the scale each gives; the analysis' reason; and the bar's actions, labelled, where today there's a key icon. Engine calls already exist: scaleChoices, functionChoices, rowNotes, insertKeyChange.
  8. Errors: a chord the app can't read gets an amber underline and the inspector says so (“Not a chord I know: Cm7#5#9x. Pick a scale for it, or check the spelling: Cm7#5#9?”) right where you typed, not in a list under the text pane.
